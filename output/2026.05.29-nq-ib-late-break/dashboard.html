what this shows

once an NQ IB break happens after 11am, how far does the initial push go (extension), and how much does it pull back from that push peak (retracement). extension is measured in multiples of the IB range from the broken level. retracement is how much price recovers from the push peak before end of session.

why post-11am is different

early breaks (10:30-11am) ride morning momentum. post-11am breaks happen after liquidity thins into the lunch window; they have a lower extension hit rate and a higher fade rate. however, breaks that do hold through lunch (past 1pm) often have a clean afternoon continuation with less noise.

key takeaway

target the first 0.5x IB range extension on the initial push; that level is hit 58% of the time. a 30-50% retrace of the initial push is normal and not a failure signal. if price fully fades to the IB (35% of late breaks), S2 activates. only 12% of late breaks reach 2x IB range.
