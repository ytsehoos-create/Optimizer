what this shows

three IB-based trade setups analyzed across 127 NQ sessions (dec 2025 - jun 2026): the break-out trade, the extension after the break, and the reversal when the IB forms against you. ES/NQ correlation data included throughout.

how to read it

the 60-minute IB is the high/low formed in the first 60 min of RTH. the 30-minute range is the ORB (opening range breakout). "IB low formed first" means the session opened with a bearish move into the IB low before establishing the IB high. "reversal" means price then broke the opposite side of the IB.

key edge

when the ORB low forms first in NQ (bearish opening move), price reverses and breaks the ORB high 75% of the time. with the true 60min IB that number rises to 81%. the same pattern in ES triggers the same NQ reversal with similar frequency — confirming ES leads NQ on IB/ORB breaks.

zone 2 — reversal setup: which side of the IB breaks first, and where does price go?

65%+ edge

when the IB low forms first in NQ (price drops and makes the session low early), the ORB high gets broken on 75% of sessions (ORB, 30min) — rising to 81% with the true 60min IB. this is the mean-reversion setup: fade the bearish open, target the range high. the same directional bias appears in ES, confirming that ES-leading-NQ is a real pattern.

both "IB low first → high breaks" and "IB high first → low breaks" are reversal setups — the key insight is that whichever side of the IB forms first, the opposite side is the higher-probability target 65-81% of the time depending on timeframe.

setup 1: fade the first IB extreme

65%+ edge
trigger

probability

75% (ORB, 30min) rising to 81% (IB, 60min)

ES confirmation

target

ORB/IB high. secondary: L1 extension above the range high (67% of ORB breakouts, 58% of IB breakouts reach L1)

stop

below the range low. if the low further extends instead of reversing, exit — this is the 25% failure case

setup 2: ORB/IB breakdown extension

65%+ edge
trigger

ORB low (30min) or IB low (60min) breaks with momentum — enter short on confirmed close below the range low

probability

85% reach L1 after ORB low breaks (30min). 72% after true IB low breaks (60min).

ES confirmation

ES breaking its own IB low simultaneously raises conviction — avoids ES leading NQ back up (the reversal case)

target

L1 (85% hit rate). L2 (57.5% hit rate) — scale out at L1, trail to L2

stop

back above the broken level. breakdown reversals (15% of ORB cases, 28% of IB cases) tend to reclaim quickly

setup 3: ORB/IB breakout continuation

borderline edge
trigger

ORB high (30min) or IB high (60min) breaks with momentum — enter long on confirmed close above the range high

probability

66.7% reach L1 on ORB breakout. 57.6% on true IB (60min) breakout — borderline, needs confirmation

ES confirmation

ES breaking IB high first and NQ following is the primary signal. standalone NQ breakout (without ES) has lower conviction

target

L1 only (67% hit rate). L2 drops to 31% — do not target L2 without strong trend-day context

caveat

ORB breakout extension odds are meaningfully weaker than breakdown odds at every timeframe. the ORB (30min) has the best breakout L1 rate at 67%; the true IB (60min) drops to 58%. require ES confirmation before trading breakout continuations

ES leads NQ on IB breaks — the correlation framework

from: ES initial balance standard, NQ initial balance standard

ES vs NQ initial balance patterns — 127 sessions | edgeful.com

the lead-lag pattern

ES and NQ show nearly identical IB reversal probabilities (72% vs 75% on "low first" with the ORB (30min)). this means ES is not generating a signal before NQ — they're running the same setup in parallel.

the edge from watching ES is confirmation: when both ES and NQ print the same IB extreme first (both IB low first), the joint signal reduces the risk of one instrument reversing while the other continues — the double-break case (30% for NQ alone).

practical use: wait for ES to break or hold an IB level before acting in NQ. if ES is still inside its IB while NQ is testing the IB extreme, the NQ move is more likely to be a fakeout.
