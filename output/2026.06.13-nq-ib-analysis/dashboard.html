what this shows

three IB-based trade setups analyzed across 127 NQ sessions (dec 2025 - jun 2026): the break-out trade, the extension after the break, and the reversal when the IB forms against you. ES/NQ correlation data included throughout.

how to read it

the 30-minute IB is the high/low formed in the first 30 min of RTH. "IB low formed first" means the session opened with a bearish move into the IB low before establishing the IB high. "reversal" means price then broke the opposite side of the IB.

key edge

when the IB low forms first in NQ (bearish opening move), price reverses and breaks the IB high 75% of the time with the 30min IB. the same pattern in ES triggers the same NQ reversal with similar frequency — confirming ES leads NQ on IB breaks.

65%+ edge

ES leads NQ on IB breaks — the correlation framework

from: ES initial balance standard, NQ initial balance standard

ES vs NQ initial balance patterns — 127 sessions | edgeful.com

the lead-lag pattern

ES and NQ show nearly identical IB reversal probabilities (72% vs 75% on "IB low first" with 30min IB). this means ES is not generating a signal before NQ — they're running the same setup in parallel.

the edge from watching ES is confirmation: when both ES and NQ print the same IB extreme first (both IB low first), the joint signal reduces the risk of one instrument reversing while the other continues — the double-break case (30% for NQ alone).

practical use: wait for ES to break or hold an IB level before acting in NQ. if ES is still inside its IB while NQ is testing the IB extreme, the NQ move is more likely to be a fakeout.
