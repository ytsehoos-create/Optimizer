what this shows

on fridays where NQ prints a large initial balance (≈1.6% of price) and then breaks the IB high, two questions: which levels get tagged after the break (levels report), and how deep does price pull back from the post-break high (retracement report).

why this filter is different

a 1.6% IB is already a wide morning range — most of the day's expected move is spent before the break. combine that with friday position-squaring and weekly-options pin risk and continuation odds drop sharply while retracement back into the IB rises. this is a mean-reversion-leaning context, not a trend one.

key takeaway

the 0.25x extension is the only high-probability level (71%); 0.5x is a coin flip (49%) and 1.0x is rare (27%). 60% of these breaks retrace ≥50% of their push and 18% fully fade back inside the IB. take the first 0.25-0.5x target, trail aggressively, and respect the fade.

how to trade it
target 0.25-0.5x, trail fast

below IBH = stand down
18% close back inside the IB; a clean break of IBH on the re-test flips the bias to a fade.
