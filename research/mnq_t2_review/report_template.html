The fills are not the weak point

T2R uses stops of about a tenth of the IB range, so I tested harsher fills. Requiring every limit to be traded through by a tick leaves $25,375. Adding two-tick worse entries and four-tick stop slippage on top leaves $24,412.

With one setting for every weekday and a flat $200 the two T2 legs still make $10,774 at PF 1.9. That is the part I would call the edge. The other $15,000 comes from per-weekday entries, stops, targets, switches and sizing that were chosen on these same 12 months, which is all the history a 5-minute chart holds. Expect live results nearer the lower bars than the top one.

The regime: how far days travel beyond their first hour

I tested about 50 daily measures of volatility, trend and recent opening-range behaviour against trade outcomes. One held up in every year: the median, over the last 20 full sessions, of the day's range divided by its IB range. I call it the regime reading. Near 1.3–1.5, days stay close to the first hour and fades at the IB edges work. Above about 1.7, days expand and fades fail.

  • T3 depends on it most. That is why it was flat for two years and good in the third.
  • T2R leans the same way but less cleanly. In the exact year it made PF 4.3 with the reading at or below 1.6 and PF 2.2 above; at 1.7 the two sides were equal. On the 15-minute yardstick its average trade was 0.25R below the 3-year median reading and about zero above.
  • T2X goes the other way, as a continuation trade should: PF 9.6 above 1.6 against 3.4 below. It is the leg that carries an expanding market.
  • IB size matters separately. T2 sits out when the IB exceeds 0.9% of price, which was 34% of days in the last 12 months against 20% before, so in volatile stretches the script is mostly T3.

Two overnight candidates

I tested five setup types (edge fade, retrace continuation, zone fade, zone momentum, breakout) over seven session windows from the Globex open to the pre-market, about 1,400 combinations, with conservative fills. In 27 of the 35 families the typical setting lost money. Fading the first hour's extremes lost in every overnight session. Two ideas survived checks on neighbouring settings, on both halves of the year and on the 5-minute file.

LX · London pullback

Range 02:00–03:00 ET, the hour before London opens. After the first break, wait for a close back inside, then rest a limit 0.30R inside the broken edge in the break's direction. Stop 0.80R inside, target 1.00R beyond. No entry after 06:00, flat at 07:00.

Stops from 0.75R to 0.90R with entries from 0.2R to 0.4R all made PF 1.35–1.7 overall and at least 1.25 in each half. Ranges ending at 03:00 were profitable at 30, 60 and 90 minutes; ranges ending at 04:00 or later were not. The 02:00–03:00 window is the best of its neighbours, which made PF 1.0–1.5, so expect less than 1.78.

GM · Globex first-hour momentum

Range 18:00–19:00 ET. If the hour closes in its top or bottom 20%, go with it at 19:00. Stop 0.85R back inside from that edge, target 0.30R beyond it. Flat at 23:00.

What to watch from here

On the 3-year yardstick, a month's P&L has no correlation with the next month's (−0.01 over 37 months), and the last 20 trades say nothing about the next one. So a good or bad month is not a signal. These are.

Regime reading

now 1.58 · normal

Above 1.7, T3 stops by itself. If it stays above 1.6 for three weeks, set the T2R dial to 0.5 and leave T2X alone. It averaged 1.32–1.42 from August to October and 1.68 so far in November.

Large-IB days

now 7 of the last 20 · watch

When more than half the last 20 sessions have an IB above 0.9%, T2 is sidelined and you are running T3 alone, the weakest leg. October and November made $469 and $340.

Drawdown from the peak

backtest worst $774

Reshuffling the year's own trades 5,000 times gives a 12-month drawdown of $1,286 at the median, $2,150 at the 95th percentile and $2,750 at the 99th. At $2,200 halve the size; at $2,750 stop and re-test.

Re-tuning

monthly

When you re-tune, also run the one-setting version. If it turns negative over the last six months while the tuned version still looks good, the tuning is doing all the work.

What this work cannot tell you

  • Exact T2R and T2X results exist only for the 12 months they were tuned on. The earlier years rest on 15-minute bars, which understate T2R. If you export the Deep Backtesting trade list for November 2023 to November 2025, I can tie the regime work to exact trades.
  • The overnight candidates have ten and a half months of history and were chosen by searching it. There is no earlier intraday data to test them on.
  • I could not compile Pine here. The day script's v3.1 additions and the overnight script each match a Python port of the same logic, and the overnight port reproduces the search engine's results trade for trade, but neither file has been through TradingView's compiler. Check the 12-month net against $29,866 (T3 on) when you load v3.1, and run both on Sim101 first. Alerts ship off in the overnight script.
  • The regime threshold was found by looking at many measures. It held in each of three years and across 10-, 20- and 40-session windows, which is why I trust it for T3, but it is evidence and not proof.
